Coverage

Our current coverage area is Carson Valley, NV including Minden, Gardnerville, and surrounding areas. With members in Reno, Dayton and Fallon.

The current goals at this time is to link Reno to Carson City. along with Dayton and extending coverage to Fallon.

Network Comments

Our network is MeshCore based, using the US Recommended preset:
  • 910.525Mhz
  • 62.5Khz Bandwidth
  • 7 Spreading Factor
  • 4/5 Coding
