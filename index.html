About Nevada Mesh

Based around Carson City, NV. We have a fully connected mesh network between Gardnerville, Minden, Carson City, Reno, Dayton, Silver Springs and Fallon.

Coverage

Our current coverage area includes Gardnerville, Minden, Carson City, Reno, Dayton, Silver Springs and Fallon.

The current goals of the mesh network are to expand coverage and improve connectivity between these areas.

Gardnerville Minden Carson City Reno Dayton Silver Springs Fallon

Network Specs

Our network is MeshCore based, using the US Recommended preset:

  • 910.525 MHz
  • 62.5 kHz Bandwidth
  • 7 Spreading Factor
  • 4/5 Coding Rate
